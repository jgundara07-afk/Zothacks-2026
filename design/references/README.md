# One Thing visual references

The proposed first-run sequence is in [`../ONBOARDING_FLOW.md`](../ONBOARDING_FLOW.md). These files are inspiration for composition, texture, hierarchy, and motion; do not reuse third-party images as app assets or copy their branding.

## AURA

Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/aura-feel-alive-again/id6738584485

`aura-appstore-01.jpg` through `aura-appstore-06.jpg` are official **App Store marketing images**, not verified screenshots of AURA's first-run onboarding. `02` is strongest for discovery cards; `04` for a progress timeline; `05` for monochrome portraiture with sparse red accents.

## Teenage Engineering

Sources: https://teenage.engineering/press/op-xy and https://teenage.engineering/press/field-system

`op-xy-05.webp` shows a human hand entering a black frame; `op-xy-06.webp` and `op-xy-08.webp` show atmospheric low-key portraiture; `op-xy-07.webp` is a high-contrast black-and-white silhouette; `op-xy-09.webp` and `field-system-03.webp` show how one red control becomes a focal point. `op-xy-04.webp` and `field-system-01.webp` are useful for precise product composition and negative space.

## User-provided photography references (October 17)

- `iris-halftone-signin-reference.png`: **Primary first-screen layout reference.** Borrow its ghosted atmospheric photograph, quiet high-contrast sign-in panel, and halftone image treatment. Adapt the composition for mobile with the photo high on the screen fading into the controls. The exact people, landscape, branding, and form details are not requirements.
- `analog-bw-storefront-grain.png`: Reference the deep blacks, visible film grain, luminous highlights, and atmospheric urban photography. Do not copy the website navigation, signage, or product branding.
- `analog-bw-editorial-portrait.png`: Reference the soft monochrome tones, subtle grain, restrained editorial composition, and generous negative space. The subject is not a required motif.

Art direction: analog, dark, photographic, and interesting without brutalist UI. Every quest/task should have a relevant, aesthetic editorial photograph with a consistent crop and legible text treatment, taking inspiration from AURA's image-led quest presentation. Use original or properly licensed images in the app; these screenshots are references only.
